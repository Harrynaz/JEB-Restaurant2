About Jeb Restaurant

Good food, genuine hospitality, and creating memorable moments.

Our Story

We believe that dining is not simply about satisfying hunger. It is about enjoying good company, experiencing excellent service, and creating memorable moments. From simple daily meals to celebratory occasions, Jeb welcomes everyone looking for high quality dining.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide fresh, delicious meals and snacks at affordable prices with heartfelt respect and attentiveness to our customers, while keeping a clean and comfortable environment.

Vision

To continually elevate our service standards and provide the most enjoyable everyday dining experience in Entebbe.

Our Core Values

1. Quality

We make every effort to prepare meals that reach and exceed our customers' tastes and preferences.

2. Customer Satisfaction

You are the reason we exist; your comfort and dining satisfaction are always our highest priority.

3. Affordability

Generous portions and different price choices ensure everyone enjoys good food without financial strain.

4. Cleanliness & Hygiene

We maintain strict hygiene and a safe, sparkling environment throughout our kitchen and dining area.

5. Respect

We treat every customer and team member with dignity, warmth, and professionalism.
